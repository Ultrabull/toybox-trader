🧸 Toybox Trader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026 · Contact: toyboxtrader.support@gmail.com

Toybox Trader ("we", "the app") is a play-money investing simulator that helps kids learn about markets. Protecting children's privacy matters to us. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices parents have. Toybox Trader uses fake money only — no real trades, purchases of securities, or real financial transactions ever occur.

Information we collect

  • Child profile: a first name or nickname, an avatar, an age, a chosen theme, and a 4-digit PIN the child sets. A parent-provided email may be entered during sign-up.
  • Gameplay data: the child's play-money portfolio, lessons completed, badges, predictions, streaks, and similar progress.
  • Family account (optional): a parent email and password, used only to sync a family's accounts across devices. The password is never stored — it is converted on the device into a key we cannot reverse.
  • Notifications (optional): if a parent enables Telegram alerts or on-device reminders, we store a Telegram chat ID and/or a browser push subscription and the device's timezone.
  • Support / bug reports (optional): if someone uses the in-app "Get Help" form, we receive the message they type, the category, and basic context (which screen, app version, device type). A grown-up may optionally add an email so we can reply and send a confirmation; that email is used only to respond to the report.
  • AI helper "Toby" (optional, paid plans): if a child uses Toby, the questions they type plus limited context (first name, age, and in-app play-money progress) are sent to our AI provider to generate a reply. We instruct the child not to share real personal details, and Toby is limited to money/learning topics. We do not use these chats for advertising or profiling.

How we use it

  • To run the app and save each child's progress.
  • To sync accounts across a family's devices when family sync is turned on.
  • To send optional reminders/alerts the parent has explicitly enabled.
  • To reply to a support or bug report when a grown-up leaves an email.

We do not show third-party ads, use third-party analytics or trackers, sell or rent personal information, or use data for advertising or profiling.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on the device (in the browser) and, when family sync is enabled, in a hosted database (Neon/PostgreSQL) accessed only through our own secure functions. The app is served over a reputable cloud hosting provider (Cloudflare). Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Service providers we use

We share the minimum data needed with trusted providers who process it only on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, and (if enabled) forwarding email sent to our support address.
  • Neon (PostgreSQL) — the synced family database.
  • Anthropic — powers the optional AI helper "Toby" (receives the child's question and limited play-progress context to generate a reply).
  • Resend — delivers support/confirmation and report emails.
  • Telegram — delivers alerts only if a parent connects it.
  • A payment processor (e.g., Stripe or an app store) — only if you subscribe; card details go directly to them, not to us.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not allow these providers to use your data for their own advertising.

Children's privacy (COPPA and similar laws)

Toybox Trader is intended for children used under the supervision of a parent or guardian, and we design it to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and comparable rules (such as the UK Children's Code and GDPR-K).

  • A parent or guardian sets up or supervises a child's use, and the parent area is behind a parental gate. By enabling the Service for a child, the parent consents to the limited collection described here.
  • We practice data minimization — we ask for a nickname (not a real name is required), an age band, and only what the features need — and we do not use children's data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond running the Service.
  • Parents can review, export, or delete their child's data at any time (see below), and can withdraw consent by deleting the account.
  • If we learn we collected a child's data without the required parental consent, we will delete it. Contact us any time about your child's information.

Parental rights & choices

  • Access/export: use the in-app "Backup code" feature to export a child's full account and progress.
  • Delete: parents can remove an individual child, or delete the entire family account and all of its data, from inside the app (Sync → Delete account). Deletion removes data from our database.
  • Contact us at toyboxtrader.support@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of your child's information.

Data retention

We keep data only while an account is active. When you delete an account, its data is removed from our database. Local device data is cleared when you delete the account or clear your browser storage.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify users in the app.
